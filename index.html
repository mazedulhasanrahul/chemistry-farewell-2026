About Our Journey

From our very first class to the countless hours spent in the Chemistry laboratory mastering titrations and organic syntheses, the Class of 2021-22 has shared an unforgettable journey. Government Titumir College has been more than an institution; it has been our second home.

This farewell is a celebration of our friendships, the guidance of our respected teachers, and the memories we have woven together. As we step into the future, we gather one last time to celebrate the end of this beautiful chapter and the beginning of forever.
